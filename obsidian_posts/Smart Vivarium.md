## Fauna
### Crested Gecko (*Correlophus ciliatus*) · [↗](https://www.google.com/search?q=crested+gecko+Correlophus+ciliatus)
![Crested gecko|250](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1YI0bc-Q7gz3E7izjZ7roAkzMVQ5S8Der)
> [!info]- Living conditions
> - Enclosure: min. 45×45×60 cm (~120 L) per adult · pair: min. 60×45×90 cm (~240 L)
> - Day temp: 22–26 °C · Night temp: 18–22 °C
> - Max safe limit: 28 °C (stress >28 °C; lethal risk >30 °C)
> - Air humidity: 50–60 % (day) · 70–80 % (after night misting)
> - Lighting/UVB: 12 h photoperiod (11–14 h seasonal); UVB 5–7 % · max UVI 1.0–2.0 at highest perch
> - Lifespan: 15–20 years
> - Adult size: 20–25 cm total · 35–55 g
> - Breeding: female ≥35–40 g and ≥18 months · 2 eggs/clutch every 4–6 weeks · 8–10 clutches/season · incubation 60–90 days at 22–26 °C · 2–3 month rest/year at 18–21 °C

> [!warning]- Risk
> - Breed only when the female is ≥35–40 g and ≥18 months old; provide a lay box and a 2–3 month rest per year.
> - Never house two males together.

### Earthworms (*Eisenia fetida*) · [↗](https://www.google.com/search?q=Eisenia+fetida+red+wiggler)
![Earthworms|250](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1eaBovTVm7krLlt5eD0a5rBdLVrSIGnWX)
> [!info]- Living conditions
> - Substrate: min. 10–15 cm deep · substrate moisture 60–80 % · pH 6.0–7.5
> - Temp: 15–25 °C (optimal 20–25 °C) · Max safe limit: 30 °C
> - Lifespan: 1–2 years (up to 4)
> - Adult size: 5–10 cm
> - Starting population: 10–20

> [!warning]- Risk
> - Needs ≥10–15 cm of soil that stays moist at the bottom, or the population dies off/migrates to the drainage layer.

### Dwarf white isopods (*Trichorhina tomentosa*) · [↗](https://www.google.com/search?q=Trichorhina+tomentosa+dwarf+white+isopod)
![Dwarf white isopods|250](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1dG4dohtJu2_svGM_WtWIqTwLMxuQLzww)
> [!info]- Living conditions
> - Temp: 21–29 °C (slow breeding <21 °C) · Max safe limit: 30 °C
> - Air humidity: 70–90 % (≥1 permanently moist substrate zone)
> - Lifespan: ~1–2 years
> - Adult size: 2–5 mm
> - Breeding: parthenogenetic (100 % female)
> - Starting population: 25–50

> [!warning]- Risk
> - Verify species on purchase: dwarf whites are 2–5 mm and pure white. Larger *Porcellio* species may nibble gecko eggs.

### Springtails (*Folsomia candida*) · [↗](https://www.google.com/search?q=Folsomia+candida+springtails)
![Springtails|250](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1c63Q5TplFcia8bL9AadeWwHxWZRRMscU)
> [!info]- Living conditions
> - Temp: 18–26 °C (optimal 21–24 °C) · Max safe limit: 28 °C
> - Humidity: moist substrate · 70–100 % RH in microhabitat
> - Lifespan: 4–8 months · generation ~3–4 weeks
> - Adult size: 1–3 mm
> - Starting population: 1 culture (hundreds)

## Flora
> [!warning]- Risk (all plants)
> - Wash roots, repot and quarantine every plant 2–4 weeks: systemic pesticides from nurseries kill isopods and springtails.

### Canopy and background
#### Pothos (*Epipremnum aureum*) · [↗](https://www.google.com/search?q=Pothos+Epipremnum+aureum)
![Pothos|250](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1ThxW-rKfRCBjKernNXMHMhw6MrL9xOzA)
> [!tip] Why
> Great at **processing nitrate** produced by the decomposers, basically **impossible to kill** and **grow pretty fast**.

> [!info]- Living conditions
> - Temp: 18–29 °C · min. 13 °C
> - Air humidity: 40–80 %
> - Light: 50–200 PPFD (µmol/m²/s)
> - Size: vines 1–2 m in enclosure (prune every 4–8 weeks)

> [!warning]- Risk
> - Fast grower: prune every 4–8 weeks or it shades the lower layers.

#### Hoya (*Hoya carnosa*, *H. kerrii*) · [↗](https://www.google.com/search?q=Hoya+carnosa+Hoya+kerrii)
![Hoya|250](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/17BhnhCYs2tB8PcW6d8AItewdNbmKqWKv)
> [!tip] Why
> Thick leaves that our Gecko can rest on and it's a very cute looking plant.

> [!info]- Living conditions
> - Temp: 18–29 °C · min. 13 °C
> - Air humidity: 40–70 %
> - Light: 100–300 PPFD
> - Substrate: dry out between waterings · Size: vines 0.5–1 m (slow growth)

> [!warning]- Risk
> - Rots in soggy substrate: mount high or on wood.

### Middle layer (sturdy perches)
#### Dwarf umbrella tree (*Schefflera arboricola*) · [↗](https://www.google.com/search?q=Dwarf+umbrella+tree+Schefflera+arboricola)
![Dwarf umbrella tree|250](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1xxZUHpD7GVQaNog_T2puj99RI9Jfqt55)
> [!info]- Living conditions
> - Temp: 16–27 °C · min. 10 °C
> - Air humidity: 40–70 %
> - Light: 100–300 PPFD
> - Size: keep 30–60 cm by pruning (natural 2–3 m)

#### Nidularium (*Nidularium* spp.) · [↗](https://www.google.com/search?q=Nidularium+bromeliad)
![Nidularium|250](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/147Tu8w8m6e0R1dnNnbEU1mbDP-OwU0a4)
> [!info]- Living conditions
> - Temp: 18–27 °C · min. 13 °C
> - Air humidity: 60–80 %
> - Light: 50–150 PPFD
> - Size: rosette 20–40 cm · tank water change 1×/week

> [!warning]- Risk
> - Flush the leaf tank weekly so water doesn't stagnate.

### Ground layer (dim, humid)
#### Lemon button fern (*Nephrolepis cordifolia* 'Duffii') · [↗](https://www.google.com/search?q=Lemon+button+fern+Nephrolepis+cordifolia+Duffii)
![Lemon button fern|250](https://gardenerspath.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/How-to-Grow-Lemon-Button-Ferns-Feature.jpg)
> [!tip] Why
> Compact replacement for Boston/Kimberly fern (same genus, ~1/3 the size); continuously replaces older fronds, which are left on the soil as leaf litter instead of pruned out.

> [!info]- Living conditions
> - Temp: 16–24 °C · min. 10 °C
> - Air humidity: 50–80 %
> - Light: 50–150 PPFD
> - Size: 20–30 cm tall and wide (slow growth)

> [!warning]- Risk
> - Prefers the cool end (≤24 °C): place low and shaded.

#### Nerve plant (*Fittonia albivenis*) · [↗](https://www.google.com/search?q=Nerve+plant+Fittonia+albivenis)
![Nerve plant|250](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1a8fZJbWjzwBS5S9W6Sb0Ww1J8vXomHqW)
> [!info]- Living conditions
> - Temp: 18–27 °C · min. 13 °C
> - Air humidity: 60–90 %
> - Light: 50–150 PPFD
> - Size: 8–15 cm tall

> [!warning]- Risk
> - Soft leaves get trampled; wilts quickly if it dries out.

#### Cushion moss (*Leucobryum* spp.) · [↗](https://www.google.com/search?q=Cushion+moss+Leucobryum)
![Cushion moss|250](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/1KBuDlmWGvtKZIpkO9ZO7eKkEE6_zQTjE)
> [!info]- Living conditions
> - Temp: 15–24 °C
> - Humidity: 70–100 % (surface constantly moist)
> - Light: 25–100 PPFD
> - Size: cushions 2–10 cm

> [!warning]- Risk
> - Needs a constantly moist surface. Place on a raised rock or cork so litter doesn't bury it and isopods don't graze it.
