StrideSense started on an Arduino board. It now runs on a
[Seeed XIAO nRF52840 Sense](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/XIAO_BLE/). This post is
the short version of why, written as a build-log note rather than a tutorial.

## The constraint that drove it

A smart insole has to disappear into a shoe. That puts hard limits on size and
on power, and it means wireless is not optional — you cannot run a cable out of
someone's shoe.

## What the board buys

- **Size.** The XIAO footprint fits where a larger dev board never could.
- **Onboard sensing.** The Sense variant brings an IMU along for the ride.
- **BLE.** The nRF52840 is a Bluetooth Low Energy part, so streaming off the
  insole is built in rather than bolted on.
