I study Business Analytics & Finance. I also spend my evenings reading boards
and writing firmware. Those two facts belong in the same paragraph, and this is
my attempt to explain why.

## The pivot

I came up through finance — a data-science bootcamp at Le Wagon in London, then
Business Analytics & Finance at Hult in Boston. Somewhere in there the problems
I wanted to work on stopped being purely financial and started being physical:
sensing, RF, autonomous systems, and fusing messy data from many sources.

So I'm moving toward the **hardware/software intersection**: embedded systems
plus data science. The Harvard Extension course on wearables and computer
vision (DGMD E-14) is part of that, and so is [StrideSense](/projects/stridesense).

## The why underneath it

My family runs a specialty coffee farm that practices regenerative agriculture.
That's where the agritech pull comes from — it isn't abstract, it's home.

The longer arc is Brazil. I want to help make Brazil more competitive in
agritech and in defense-adjacent technology. That's the mission that makes the
late nights with a multimeter make sense.
