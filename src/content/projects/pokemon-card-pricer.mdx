A computer-vision pipeline that recognizes Pokémon trading cards from images or
Whatnot livestream footage and estimates a market price with a confidence
range. Paired with a six-episode vertical video series documenting the build.

## Problem

Prices on the secondary market move fast and depend on set, rarity, and
condition. During a livestream there's no time to look anything up — you want an
instant read with an honest sense of how sure the estimate is.

## Approach

- Detect and identify the card from a frame (image or livestream capture).
- Map the identification to a price estimate, reported as a range rather than a
  single number so the uncertainty is visible.
- Document the whole thing as a 6-episode vertical video series.
