A retrieval-augmented-generation pipeline that runs entirely on a local server —
Ollama for the model, ChromaDB for the vector store, and LangChain to wire it
together — aimed at surfacing corruption signals in government documents.

## Problem

Public-document corpora are large and dull to read. The interesting patterns
are buried. Sending sensitive documents to a hosted API is also the wrong move —
this needed to stay local.

## Approach

- **Local model** via Ollama, so nothing leaves the machine.
- **ChromaDB** as the vector store for the document chunks.
- **LangChain** to orchestrate retrieval and querying.
