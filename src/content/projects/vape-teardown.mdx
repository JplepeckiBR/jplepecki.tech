Disposable vapes are small, cheap, mass-produced embedded systems that people
throw away. I tore one down and reverse-engineered its battery-protection
circuit purely as a learning exercise — a cheap way to practice reading real
hardware.

## Why

The skills transfer directly to StrideSense: reading a board, tracing a
protection circuit, and reasoning about a small battery-powered device are
exactly what a wearable needs.
